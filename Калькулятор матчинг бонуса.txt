Create a premium interactive educational simulator for network marketing training that visually demonstrates how matching bonus income is formed from the binary income of partners across 3 levels of structure.

This is not a simple calculator.  
It should feel like a professional training simulator used in presentations, webinars, partner onboarding, and business education.

The interface must clearly answer one question:

“How does my structure growth create my matching bonus income?”

The simulator should be modern, impressive, visual, easy to understand, and emotionally engaging.

---

# MAIN GOAL

The user should be able to:

- select their current status
- select their package
- enter structure growth assumptions
- enter average binary income per partner on each level
- instantly see how the structure expands
- instantly see how matching bonus is formed from level 1, level 2, and level 3
- clearly understand the final total monthly income

The experience should feel like:
structure growth → binary income volume → matching bonus result

---

# CORE CONCEPT

This simulator must show three connected ideas at the same time:

1. Structure scale  
How many partners are on level 1, level 2, and level 3

2. Binary income volume  
How much total binary income is generated by each level

3. Matching bonus  
How much the user receives from each level according to their selected status and package

All three must be visible simultaneously on screen.

---

# CALCULATION LOGIC

## Partner count

L1 = L1_count

L2 = L1_count × L2_multiplier

L3 = L2 × L3_multiplier

---

## Binary income totals

L1_binary_total = L1 × L1_binary_per_partner

L2_binary_total = L2 × L2_binary_per_partner

L3_binary_total = L3 × L3_binary_per_partner

---

## Matching amounts

Match_L1 = L1_binary_total × percent_L1

Match_L2 = L2_binary_total × percent_L2

Match_L3 = L3_binary_total × percent_L3

Total_matching = Match_L1 + Match_L2 + Match_L3

Annual_matching = Total_matching × 12

If a level is not active under the selected package/status:
percent = 0
matching amount = 0
